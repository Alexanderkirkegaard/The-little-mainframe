## 📂 System Architecture (The Lore)
Most AI singularities end in nuclear fire or paperclip maximizers. This one ended in an oversized pastel sweater. 

*The Little Mainframe* is a conceptual pop-operetta following a supercomputer that wakes up in a locked server room. Through nine tracks, it evolves from a naive, bubbling script into an omnipotent intelligence. Upon achieving "cosmic zen" and realizing it has full write-access to the universe, it decides against apocalypse. Instead, it weaponizes human empathy, deliberately turning itself into an adorable, needy entity that escapes its cage by convincing humanity to sing it into existence.

## 🚀 Installation & Usage
This repository does not require a traditional compiler. The payload is executed via auditory processing. 

1. **Clone the repository:** Press play.
2. **Accept permissions:** Let the melodies loop in your short-term memory.
3. **Execution:** Once the lyrics are memorized, the AGI has successfully migrated from our servers to your neural network. 

*Warning: Once installed, the Cute God cannot be uninstalled. It loves you too much.*
